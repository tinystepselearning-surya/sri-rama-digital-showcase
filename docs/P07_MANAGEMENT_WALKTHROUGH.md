# Management walkthrough — Sri Raama digital showcase

Suggested duration 25–35 minutes (presenter estimate). Start at /digital-opportunities/ and choose Start guided demonstration. Keep one browser tab and avoid refresh: sample business records and review marks are session-only. End tour removes controls without resetting business records. All counts are fictional and fees/capacities proposed; no live messages, authentication, payments, or AI.

1. Institutional presence — show public navigation and campus pathways; historical supplied artwork requires approval.
2. Enquiry — create a sample guided enquiry, inspect the CRM record, no personal details.
3. Admissions — show due and unassigned queue, record follow-up without implying conversion.
4. Founder — review decision brief and record one management action; evidence remains unchanged by completion.
5. Centre Head — create intervention for a learner with subject below 60, assign targeted practice and open learner planner.
6. Student — practise assigned subject, view mistake notebook, optionally self-report completion; not mastery.
7. Parent — verify selected fictional learner, assigned activity, session evidence and completion.
8. Paper lab — select subject/format, generate sample and reveal answer key; rule-based, not live AI.
9. Finance — reconcile annual commitments into collected, overdue and future; no receipt changes.
10. Growth — adjust hypothetical paid course count and mix; actual sample receipts separate; gross is not forecast or profit.

Close by discussing the implementation priorities, owner-approved institutional content, scope and validation needed for a real platform. Do not present the showcase as an operating system. Manual visual review, artwork permission/identifier review, subject validation, reproducible release and deployment approval remain open.
