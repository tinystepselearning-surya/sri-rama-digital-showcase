# Educational quality framework — P02 extension
User direction: improve educational substance, approach and quality continuously, not merely public copy. Scope: existing institutional teaching, stream and support pages; P05 still covers complete sample question/answer review, chapter library and mock quality.
Evidence-informed design sources consulted 27 Nov 2026: https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1 (worked examples, independent problems, retrieval and spaced study); https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition (explicit modelling, scaffolded planning and self-regulation). These support the proposed framework, not institutional implementation claims.
Implemented: five-stage diagnostic-to-revision teaching cycle on approach index; original worked examples, independent challenge, revealable reasoning, misconception and delayed revision prompts for MPC, BiPC, MEC, CEC; five methodology and three support pages with distinct applied examples and links. Four previews are introductory not exam-standard comprehensive material. No fake mastery scores, guarantees, faculty names, or live data. No route, pricing or backend changes.
Acceptance: accurate examples and clear distinction from established operations; static regression verifies page content; browser/layout checks required for exact candidate. P05 continues education quality with subject reviewer validation recommended before production instruction. No deployment.
