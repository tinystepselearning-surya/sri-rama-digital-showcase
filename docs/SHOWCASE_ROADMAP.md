# Showcase experience roadmap

## Public site first
Nine named published campus/accommodation entries, each with a dedicated profile; addresses sourced from https://sriraama.in/contact-us/ and campus types cross-checked against homepage/footer https://sriraama.in/. Gokul entries share address; Miyapur A/C gender designation disputed. No live map pin or facility claims. Campus-level programme allocations and actual faculty roster pending. Four exam pathways each have proposed short and long course information, not confirmed current offerings. Faculty role profiles are placeholders; replace with individual approved coach records. Five proposed methodology and parent support pages, plus existing parent journal and founder page.

## Connected demo workspaces
Use a shared deterministic synthetic dataset with stable campus, learner, assessment, lead and enrolment IDs. All workspaces have direct URLs and direct navigation. No login, credential form, role selector or authentication gate. No real data or network writes. Maintain a consistent selected campus and date window. Founder view: enrolments, attendance (present / scheduled eligible attendance), assessment participation and comparable scores (same examination, max marks and cohort), top learners overall and by campus, improvement, low-attendance follow-up, online enquiry vs walk-in leads, qualified leads, visits, applications, confirmed enrolments, conversion by mature cohort, ageing, staff workload and source quality. Illustrative revenue only; no causal ROI claims. Admissions staff: create online/walk-in sample leads, ownership, stages, tasks, next contact date, notes, duplicate indication, campus routing, follow-up and conversion; parent view: only selected synthetic learner, timetable, attendance, marks and feedback, progress, upcoming tests, notices, support requests. Student view: study plan, practice, results and resources. Campus coordinator view optional: campus-scoped attendance/academic and admissions queues. Preserve the existing sample test and enquiry-to-CRM flow; replace isolated mock counters with shared synthetic records in the next phase. No production auth, Firebase database, AI or payment setup for showcase. Build and browser verification required before hosting.


## First direct-access analytics implementation
Founder, admissions, campus, teacher and parent workspace routes now use a common fictional MPC learner dataset and shared in-memory lead records; existing student workspace remains directly linked. Three completed sample weeks, same 300-mark assessment framework, weighted attendance, prior-week differences, subject and score distribution, top scores/improvement, exceptions, online/walk-in snapshots and editable lead stages. Admissions snapshot deliberately does not inherit academic reporting-week filters. All numbers fictional. Teacher/campus views currently read-only; parent learner selector is a demo convenience, not access control. More detailed follow-up tasks, cohort funnel, fee and staffing insights, drill-through, cross-filtering, notifications and student portal expansion remain planned. Build and browser verification pending.
