# P01 — Metric definitions and reconciliation
All records fictional. Academic months May–October 2026; current CRM/action snapshot 27 November 2026; annual fee year May 2026–April 2027.
| Metric | Source / calculation | Scope and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Enrolled students | unique learner IDs | fixed current roster, not CRM conversions or historical month-end headcount |
| Selected cohort | learner filter campus, stream, year, batch | academic analysis only |
| Attendance | present sessions / (30 × learners) | selected month; aggregate weighted by learner count |
| Mean assessment | sum of three subject marks / (300 × learners) | comparable stream/year common practice, not official board marks |
| Attendance exception | fewer than 24 of 30 sessions | sample review threshold, not legal attendance requirement |
| Subject review | any mark below 60/100 | sample management criterion, not diagnosis |
| Score change | current minus preceding score | marks for individual; percentage points for rate; no prior value in May |
| Seat capacity | hypothetical campus-stream-year allocation | available = positive cohort vacancies; excess counted separately |
| Annual campus fees | gross less concession | one obligation per learner; fixed October roster |
| Collections | cumulative recorded receipts through cutoff | monthly instalments, not monthly billing |
| Overdue | max(0, cumulative scheduled less receipts) | no future payments included |
| Future scheduled | annual obligation minus cumulative schedule | existing commitments, no revenue forecast |
| Remaining annual balance | annual less receipts | equals overdue + future for sample ledger |
| Enquiry count | lead IDs in selected business and filters | current snapshot; creation-month history distinct |
| Active | not Converted or Closed | current status |
| Converted | current Converted records | not admission roster or cash receipt |
| Early conversion | first conversion before next month among creation-month cohort | not fixed-duration or causal funnel |
| Due follow-up | open, not marked followed-up, due on/before 27 Nov | current snapshot |
| Paid online orders | confirmed initial digital conversions with catalogue product | immutable historical sample order set, independent of later CRM edits |
| Online receipts | order price in conversion month | single shared confirmed-order source for Finance and Growth |
| Visits and active-user months | illustrative aggregate counts | no measured attribution; active-user months may repeat people |
| Practice, mock, flashcard activity | illustrative historical aggregates; separate live session events | no implied conversion funnel |
| Open actions | task scope/kind/status | completion not outcome; sample task dates are current snapshot |
| Learner progress | selected fictional ID, shared records and personal events | session only; refresh resets |
