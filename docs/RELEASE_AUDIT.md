# Showcase release audit — 27 November 2026
Scope: independent demonstration, no login, backend, payments or personal data capture. Not a production ERP or official institutional website.
## Findings and decision gates
| Area | Finding / required action |
|---|---|
| Public brand | Results-led homepage, dedicated exam pages, campus profiles and guides. Historical poster dates, actual rankings, faculty bios and specific campus facilities require institutional sign-off. |
| Open learning | All tests, score reports, questions, flashcards free; no mandatory lead. 5 mini-mock tracks; 18 shared authored questions and 12 cards are samples, not official or full-length exam banks. |
| Commercial | Only 8 paid coaching products. Exclude free resources from finance. Growth scenario assumptions are hypothetical; no measured conversions. |
| CRM | Sample enquiries, pipeline, tasks and profiles. History is current-state based, not causal attribution; historical online orders independent of leads. |
| Academics | 2304 synthetic learners, six months, four streams; attendance is learner-session aggregate, not daily register. Comparable cohort rankings only. |
| Financial | Roster-derived campus charges; paid digital aggregates separate; cash collections not recognised revenue. No cost, refunds, taxation or profit model. |
| Operations | Teacher edits and parent readback in shared app memory; no real messages, scheduling engine, durable database, auth, or multi-user state. |
| Privacy | Public repo includes raw supplied posters with hall-ticket identifiers. Hosting media allowlist is not repository privacy. Owner should restrict repository visibility or review redaction and history; no automatic removal or permission changes. Do not attach real records to these open pages. |
| Engineering | Prior candidate build/static pass; browser stopped at founder mobile overflow. Responsive correction and expanded checks added; latest candidate requires successful CI and visual review. No Firebase deployment. |
| Opportunity | Free learning acquisition, campus enquiry conversion, online annual/crash growth, learner support, fee recovery, parent retention; future distribution via partnerships and institution-approved content. Production data collection, analytics, identity, safeguarding, security and consent require separate approval. |
